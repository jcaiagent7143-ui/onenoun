# Market entry review

Always pull the last four fiscal years from the filings before you start.

When you size the opportunity, work out the whole spend of everyone who could
conceivably buy, then narrow it to the part your product can actually serve,
then narrow again to the slice you could realistically win in three years.
Report all three, never just the largest.

Assess the industry with Porter's Five Forces.

Lay out a SWOT analysis (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats).

Prioritize initiatives with RICE scoring.

Write objectives as SMART goals.

Remember Conway's Law when reviewing integration plans.

Escalate to the committee chair if the deal exceeds the mandate.
